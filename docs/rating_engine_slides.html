Model · Base

Standard Elo

Expected(A) = 1 / (1 + 10(RB − RA) / 400)
ΔA = K × (ActualA − ExpectedA)
  • Every athlete starts at 1500.
  • Actual is 1 for a win, 0 for a loss, and 0.5 for a tie.
  • Surprising results create larger updates than expected results.
Everything that follows solves a limitation of plain two-player Elo.

Model · Addition 1

A whole finishing field

Each entry is compared pairwise with every valid opponent.

Actuali = mean(Actualij) · Expectedi = mean(Expectedij)
Δi = Keffective × (Actuali − Expectedi)
  • Finish time determines each pairwise win, loss, or tie.
  • Averaging prevents field size from multiplying the raw update.
  • Entries sharing an athlete are not compared.
  • All pre-race ratings are frozen until every update is calculated.

Model · Addition 2

Crews and team boats

Rteam = mean(Rathletes)
Δathlete = Δteam × 1 / √crew_size
100%K1 athlete update
71%K2 / C2 update
50%K4 / C4 update

If an athlete legitimately appears more than once in a merged group, their pending updates are averaged before application.

Model · Addition 3

Four distance ratings

Every athlete carries a base rating for super sprint, sprint, fondo, and marathon.

The raced class receives the direct update. Nearby classes receive limited transfer, reduced further when already well established.

Model · Addition 4

Discipline and boat context

Effective rating = distance rating + discipline modifier + boat modifier

The athlete's primary discipline and boat are the zero-point reference. Other contexts learn offsets.

Modifiers

Shared distance skill plus context offsets. Current default.

Ignore

Distance skill only; no discipline or boat effect.

Independent

Separate distance ratings for every context.

Model · Addition 5

Confidence controls learning

Uncertainty(n) = λ / (λ + effective evidence)
  • Distance ratings use λ = 10; context modifiers use λ = 5.
  • Updates are allocated across distance, discipline, and boat according to uncertainty.
  • The update also receives a 1× to 2× multiplier based on the highest active uncertainty.
  • New components learn quickly; established components stabilize.

Model · Addition 6

Field-size-aware K

Keff = K × clip[(entries / 20)0.25, 0.70, 1.75]
21minimum Keff
30Keff at 20 entries
52.5maximum Keff

Large fields carry more ranking information, but the shallow exponent and hard limits prevent mass events from dominating history.

Model · Addition 7

Inactivity and changing specialism

Inactivity

  • Rating points do not decay.
  • Evidence decays after 365 days.
  • Evidence half-life: 730 days.

Primary context

  • Switch after a challenger leads by 10 races.
  • Rebase ratings and modifiers.
  • Effective ratings remain unchanged.
The engine separates estimated skill from confidence in that estimate.

Final engine

One race through the system

Group + clean→Decay stale evidence→Rate crews→Pairwise outcomes
Field delta→Crew + uncertainty scale→Allocate + transfer→Apply together

Every update is calculated from frozen pre-race state, eliminating within-group update-order bias.

Validation

Evaluation without look-ahead

Predict first → score the prediction → update afterward

Accuracy

Was the higher-rated entry actually faster?

Brier score

Squared probability error. Lower is better.

Log loss

Penalizes confident wrong predictions. Lower is better.

Model selection

The search winner is not automatically the production winner

Best strict prediction

  • 2,000 sampled configurations
  • 78.54% strict accuracy
  • 0.1408 Brier
  • 0.4362 log loss

The tradeoff

  • K = 300
  • All-to-all distance transfer
  • Grouped accuracy falls to 72.10%
  • Rating standard deviation: 383 vs 123
Use a held-out time period and explicit population-stability constraints before changing the operating configuration.

Interpretation

What the rating means

It means

Expected relative finishing strength in a specified distance, discipline, and boat context, based on historical opponents.

It does not mean

An absolute time prediction, a causal measure of talent, or a guarantee in an unobserved context.

  • Metadata parsing and athlete-ID consistency remain important dependencies.
  • Crew interaction is not modeled beyond mean athlete strength.
  • Average rating is not strictly conserved after crew and uncertainty scaling.
